Autonomous-driving stack

A self-driving car handles the roadopen →
A fixed 2 m gap becomes a crash; the speed-dependent gap holds
Detect the vehicleFuse sensors by their noise: a camera pins the bearing, a radar pins the range, and inverse-covariance fusion is sharp in both
Track it over timeGate every detection against the track's own prediction
Predict where it goesPredict with the motion the object is actually doing: a constant-velocity guess leaves the road the moment the car is turning
Keep a safe gapKeep a following gap that grows with speed (reaction + braking distance)
Hold the laneSteer on heading error plus cross-track (Stanley), not cross-track alone, or lane-keeping overshoots and weaves
