Can we add RSS & CoRL 2025?

Short answer: not for 2025 yet — the proceedings aren't published to any source I can reach. But the 2024 editions of both are available right now. Here's the full picture and your options.

The goal

Add RSS (Robotics: Science and Systems) and CoRL (Conference on Robot Learning) 2025 to the explorer, using the same pipeline that worked for ICRA and IROS 2025 — a complete paper list, plus each paper's abstract, then the per-paper and theme analysis.

The verdict

RSS 2025 and CoRL 2025 don't exist in any source I can reach yet. It's a timing gap, not a capability gap — both conferences happened, but their machine-readable proceedings haven't been published/indexed. RSS 2025 will appear in DBLP eventually; CoRL 2025 will appear on PMLR (likely early 2026-style lag).
