We ran the methods — not just read them

For ten of the standout 2025 papers we cloned the real code and ran it ourselves, on GPUs and laptop CPUs, to produce the results below. These are our own outputs, not figures lifted from the papers. Each links to the theme where it lives. ⌂ Home

Reading a method tells you what it claims; running it tells you what it does. Every visual here came out of a real run — a self-driving stack turning six camera feeds into a plan, a colon rebuilt from endoscope video, thirty-two robots threading a maze. Where a paper's weights were locked away, we say so and use an honest stand-in.
